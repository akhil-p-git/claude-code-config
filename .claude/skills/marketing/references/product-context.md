# Product context doc

One shared file every marketing and product task reads first. Pattern adapted from coreyhaines31/marketingskills `product-marketing` (MIT).

## Where
`.agents/product-marketing.md` in the product's repo (the path coreyhaines31/marketingskills reads, so its skills find it too). Keep a `Version` line and a newest-first `Changelog` at the bottom.

## How to build it
1. Auto-draft from the repo: README, landing/marketing pages, package.json/pyproject description, pricing page, docs, changelog, issue tracker themes. Quote the product's own words where possible.
2. Mark anything inferred as **Assumed** and every gap as `[NEED: …]`. Ask the user only the questions the repo can't answer, numbered, each with your best guess.
3. Prefer verbatim customer language (reviews, support tickets, interview notes, Reddit/HN threads) over your paraphrase — it's the raw material for copy.

## Sections
1. **Product** — what it is in one sentence; category; what it is *not*.
2. **Audience** — who buys/uses it; segments; the trigger moment that makes them look for a solution.
3. **Personas** — 1–3, each with job title/context, goals, frustrations, and how they evaluate tools.
4. **Problems** — the painful problems it solves, in the customer's words, ranked by severity.
5. **Competitive alternatives** — what they'd do without you: direct competitors, adjacent tools, spreadsheets/manual work, doing nothing. Why each falls short *for this audience*.
6. **Differentiation** — capabilities the alternatives lack, and the value each enables (with proof).
7. **Objections and anti-personas** — reasons people don't buy; who it is not for.
8. **Switching dynamics** (JTBD forces) — push of the current situation, pull of the new solution, anxiety about switching, habit of the old way.
9. **Customer language** — verbatim phrases for pains, outcomes, and alternatives.
10. **Brand voice** — 3–5 voice constants (e.g. plain, precise, dry humor) and how tone flexes by channel; words to avoid.
11. **Proof points** — metrics, logos, quotes, case studies, awards — each with its source. Nothing unsourced.
12. **Goals** — current business goal (signups, activation, revenue), primary conversion, and constraints (budget, time, channels).
